On the laptop (Table~\ref{tab:speed}), whisper.cpp Q8\_0 is as fast as Q4\_0, as accurate as F16 and half its size: full small is a 252\,MB file running at 0.57 times real time with 20.3\% MER. The language-ID head raises base's real-time factor from 0.21 to 0.30 (CTranslate2) and re-ranking every clip to 1.76; gating, which re-ranks about a quarter of clips, gives an estimated 0.7.
