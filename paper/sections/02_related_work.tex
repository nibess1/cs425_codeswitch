\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Whisper on code-switched speech.}
Whisper~\citep{radford2023whisper} is trained on weakly labelled multilingual speech. Fine-tuning it on code-switched corpora lowers its error rate considerably~\citep{yang2024adapting,zhou2025csdialogue}. Even at full precision, it can drop embedded words or output their translation~\citep{peng2023prompting}. These studies report one overall error rate.

\paragraph{Scoring and training for embedded words.}
PIER~\citep{ugan2025pier} computes the error rate only on words of interest, such as the code-switched words, and shows that an overall rate can improve while those words get worse. \citet{ugan2025adapting} give tokens at switch points extra weight in training. Minimum word error rate training~\citep{prabhavalkar2018mwer} lowers the expected error over a set of candidate transcripts instead of the error of each token.

\paragraph{Quantizing speech models.}
Weights can be stored in 8 bits~\citep{dettmers2022llmint8} or in 4 bits with NF4, and low-rank adapters~\citep{hu2022lora} can be trained on top of a 4-bit model~\citep{dettmers2023qlora}. For Whisper, \citet{sohler2026quantizing} study how quantization choices affect error, and \citet{ferraz2024efficient} find that compression hurts low-resource languages more. On-device runtimes such as CTranslate2~\citep{ctranslate2} and whisper.cpp~\citep{whispercpp} have their own formats. None of these studies looks at code-switched speech.

\paragraph{What we change.}
We start from Whisper fully fine-tuned on code-switched speech, as in \citet{yang2024adapting} and \citet{zhou2025csdialogue}, and add:
\begin{itemize}
  \item scoring by three roles (embedded, matrix next to a switch, other matrix), where PIER scores the words of interest only;
  \item separate loss weights for embedded words and for matrix words next to a switch, where \citet{ugan2025adapting} weight switch points;
  \item a minimum-risk loss that forgives correct translations, where MWER counts every word alike;
  \item re-ranking with a frame-level language-ID head, used only when a switch is heard;
  \item a measure of how much more quantization hurts embedded words, in on-device formats, and two repairs.
\end{itemize}
