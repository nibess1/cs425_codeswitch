\paragraph{Real voices are easier than synthetic ones, and RQ1 holds.}
Table~\ref{tab:human} scores the phone recordings (\S\ref{sec:data}) exactly as the corpora. Fine-tuned models make 15--27 points fewer embedded errors here than on the same sentences in synthetic voices (full small 27.8\% vs.\ 54.6\%), which mispronounce embedded words. The role pattern is unchanged: off-the-shelf small gets 83.9\% of embedded units wrong but only 23.5\% of other matrix units, and writes 48\% of embedded units as sound-alikes in the other script; fine-tuning cuts embedded error by 52.5 points [39.8, 65.3]. Matrix units next to a switch are harder than other matrix units in four of five fine-tuned models (full small: 12.4\% vs.\ 9.0\%). Embedded Mandarin is harder here than embedded English (full small 33.8\% vs.\ 18.5\%), but those sentences carry the rarer drug terms. With 227 embedded units, the full system and plain fine-tuning are within noise of each other (small, embedded: $-$3.6 [$-$10.0, 2.7]).

\paragraph{4-bit hurts embedded words even for small.}
8-bit is again safe: no 8-bit setting in any backend changes embedded error by an interval that excludes 0 (Figure~\ref{fig:human}). At 4-bit, embedded error rises by 7--9 points for both base models (ft base Q4\_0: +8.9 [2.9, 15.4]) and, unlike on the corpora, for full small in NF4 (+7.6 [2.6, 13.3]), while matrix error mostly does not change measurably (full small $-$1.4 [$-$3.1, 0.3]). So $R$ is mostly not reportable; ft base Q4\_0 gives $R=3.0$ [0.9, 8.8]. whisper.cpp Q4\_0 keeps small within 1.2 points. Tiny is dominated by repetition loops on single clips (without the worst NF4 clip its embedded change is +0.5), so we draw no conclusion for it.

\paragraph{Example outputs.}
Table~\ref{tab:examples} shows 16 recordings drawn at random. Off-the-shelf small keeps most embedded English when the sentence is Mandarin-main (insulin, module, release) but loses embedded Mandarin in English-main sentences, writing it as English sound-alikes (\zh{身份证}$\rightarrow$``shuffling jump'', \zh{贷款}$\rightarrow$``Taekwun''). Fine-tuned full small fixes most of these but still fails on rare terms (\zh{布洛芬}$\rightarrow$\zh{不落分}, \zh{会议室}$\rightarrow$``hui yi shah'', prednisolone, polyclinic$\rightarrow$polytechnic) and once repeats a phrase instead of the sentence. In NF4 most outputs are unchanged; it adds errors on \zh{身份证} (written \zh{身分证}) and \zh{期末考}, and happens to avoid the repeated phrase.
